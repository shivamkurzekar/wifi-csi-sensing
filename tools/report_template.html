RSSI over time

Overall received signal strength. A sharp, sustained drop usually means something physically obstructed the line of sight between the ESP32 and the router (e.g. someone walking directly between them).

CSI variability over time

The spread (std-dev) of amplitude across all active subcarriers in each packet. A stable, empty environment keeps this low and flat; movement near the signal path perturbs many subcarriers at once and pushes this up. Shaded bands mark periods that ran well above the first-15s baseline.
